# QuizForge - Quick Start Guide

## 🎯 What This App Does

QuizForge automatically generates exam-style multiple choice questions from any textbook chapter or study material you upload. **No manual formatting required!**

### Key Features:
- ✅ Upload PDF or text files (any textbook content)
- ✅ AI automatically generates questions
- ✅ Print paper worksheets OR take digital quizzes
- ✅ Each student gets unique, randomized questions
- ✅ Auto-grading for digital quizzes
- ✅ Answer keys for paper exams

---

## 👨‍🏫 For Teachers

### Step 1: Upload Your Textbook Chapter

1. Click **"My Chapters"** in the left sidebar
2. Click **"Add New Chapter"**
3. Fill in:
   - **Title:** e.g., "Chapter 16: The Era of Reconstruction"
   - **Subject:** e.g., "US History"
4. **Upload your content:**
   - Click **"Upload File (PDF or Text)"**
   - Select your PDF textbook chapter OR text file
   - OR paste the text directly into the box
5. Click **"Save Chapter"**

**That's it!** The app now has your content and is ready to generate questions.

### Step 2: Generate a Quiz

1. Click **"Generate Quiz"** in the left sidebar
2. Select your chapter from the dropdown
3. Choose number of questions (5-50)
4. Click **"Generate Quiz with AI"**
5. Wait 5-15 seconds while AI creates questions
6. Quiz is automatically created!

### Step 3: Choose How to Administer the Quiz

#### Option A: Print Paper Worksheet (for in-class exams)

1. After quiz is generated, you'll see the questions
2. Click **"Print"** button
3. A print preview opens with:
   - Student name/date fields
   - All questions with A/B/C/D choices
   - Space for circling answers
4. Print copies for your class
5. **Answer key prints on separate page** (teacher copy only)
6. Students complete on paper
7. Grade using the answer key

#### Option B: Digital Quiz (for homework or computer lab)

1. Share the quiz with students (they can access it on their devices)
2. Students click **"Generate Quiz"** and select the same chapter
3. **Each student gets unique, randomized questions**
4. Students answer questions on their device
5. Click **"Submit Quiz"**
6. **Auto-graded instantly!**
7. Students see their score and can review explanations

### Step 4: Review Results (Digital Quizzes)

After students submit digital quizzes:
- See their score immediately
- Review which questions they got right/wrong
- Read explanations for each answer
- See source text references
- Retake the quiz for practice

## 👨‍🎓 For Students

### Taking a Digital Quiz

1. Click **"Generate Quiz"** in the left sidebar
2. Select the chapter your teacher assigned
3. Choose number of questions (if given a choice)
4. Click **"Generate Quiz with AI"**
5. **You get unique questions** (different from other students!)
6. Read each question carefully
7. Click your answer (A, B, C, or D)
8. Continue through all questions
9. Click **"Submit Quiz"**
10. See your score immediately!

### Reviewing Your Results

After submitting:
- See which questions you got right ✓
- See which questions you got wrong ✗
- Read explanations for correct answers
- Review the source text from your textbook
- Click **"Retake Quiz"** to practice again

### Paper Worksheet (if teacher prints it)

1. Your teacher gives you a printed worksheet
2. Write your name and date at the top
3. Read each question
4. **Circle** the letter of your answer (A, B, C, or D)
5. Turn in to your teacher
6. Teacher grades with answer key
7. Get it back with your score

## ❓ Common Questions

### Q: Do I need to format my content in a special way?
**A:** NO! Just upload your textbook chapter as-is. The AI understands any educational content.

### Q: Can I upload scanned PDFs (images of text)?
**A:** No, the PDF must have selectable text. If you can highlight and copy the text, it will work.

### Q: Will every student get the same questions?
**A:** No! Each student gets unique, randomized questions. This prevents cheating.

### Q: Can students see the correct answers?
**A:** Yes, after submitting, students see:
- Which answers were correct
- Explanations for each question
- Source text references

### Q: Can I print the answer key for students?
**A:** You can, but it's marked "TEACHER COPY - DO NOT DISTRIBUTE" to remind you.

### Q: How long does it take to generate a quiz?
**A:** Usually 5-15 seconds, depending on content length and internet speed.

### Q: Do students need accounts?
**A:** No! Anyone can use QuizForge without creating an account.

### Q: Is my data private?
**A:** Yes! All data is stored locally in your browser. Nothing is sent to external servers (except the content for AI processing, which is temporary).

### Q: Can I use this for free?
**A:** Yes! QuizForge is completely free. No subscriptions, no API keys, no hidden costs.

## 📞 Need Help?

### Troubleshooting

**"Failed to generate quiz"**
- Check your internet connection
- Try again (AI service may be temporarily unavailable)
- Make sure content is at least 200 characters

**"PDF won't upload"**
- Make sure PDF has selectable text (try highlighting text in the PDF)
- Try converting to text file
- Copy-paste the text instead

**"Questions seem wrong"**
- AI sometimes makes mistakes
- Try regenerating the quiz
- Review questions before giving to students
- Use more detailed content

### Best Practices

**For Teachers:**
1. Always test-generate a quiz before assigning to students
2. Review questions for accuracy
3. Have a backup plan (regenerate if needed)
4. Consider printing extra copies
5. Keep answer keys secure

**For Students:**
1. Read questions carefully
2. Don't rush through the quiz
3. Review explanations for wrong answers
4. Use source references to study
5. Retake quizzes multiple times
